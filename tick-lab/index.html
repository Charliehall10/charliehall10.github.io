About Tick Lab

Tick Lab is a practice tool for tick manipulation: click rhythms that make gathering skills roll more often than they normally would. Mistakes here cost nothing, and the replay shows exactly which tick each click landed on and why a roll was lost.

How the simulation works

The server runs a 600ms tick. Each tick it processes your clicks first, then your turn: stalls ending, queued actions, interaction with objects, movement, then interaction with NPCs. A hidden skilling timer counts down every tick, and gathering only rolls on the tick it reaches zero.

Your clicks reach the server half your ping after you make them, and are processed on the next tick after they arrive. Set your real ping so the timing matches the game.

Sources

The tick model follows the OSRS Wiki's Tick manipulation page and the community guide A Guide For Tick Manipulation Mechanics In Skilling (data-dependent/osrs-guides), which documents each method tick by tick. All art and sound are original.
